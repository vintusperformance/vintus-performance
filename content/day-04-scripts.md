# Day 4 Scripts — Executive Performance

**Angle:** Most executives are running the fitness equivalent of no strategic plan at all
**Platforms:** IG + TikTok
**Format:** Talking-head, direct to camera
**Hook types covered:** blunt statement, direct question, scenario, number-framed, contrarian claim, analogy

Distinct from Day 1 (which was about *budgeting* time) and Day 2 (which was about *deciding at 6am*). This one is about strategy versus activity — having no destination, no sequence, and no review.

Read once, speak into the camera. No stage directions, no hashtags.

---

### 1. Blunt statement hook

You have a three-year plan for the business.

You have no plan at all for the body that has to survive it.

Not a bad plan. Not an outdated plan. None.

You have activity. Activity isn't strategy.

---

### 2. Direct question hook

What is your training supposed to accomplish by December?

Not "get in shape." What specifically.

If you can't answer that, you don't have a program.

You have a habit. Habits maintain. Programs move you somewhere.

---

### 3. Scenario hook

Someone walks into your board meeting and presents the plan.

The plan is: work hard, stay consistent, see what happens.

You'd have them out of the room in ten minutes.

That's the plan you've been running on your own health for years.

---

### 4. Number-framed hook — sharpest of the set, lead with this one

I write in four-week blocks. Each block has exactly one job.

Build a base. Add strength. Sharpen conditioning. Recover.

You always know which block you're in and what it's for.

Most people train fifty-two weeks a year with zero blocks and zero jobs.

That's not discipline. That's motion.

---

### 5. Contrarian claim hook

You're not undertrained. You're unplanned.

You show up more than most people ever will.

But consistency pointed at nothing is just expensive activity.

Direction first. Then the consistency actually compounds.

---

### 6. Analogy hook

Every function in your company has a quarterly objective.

Sales knows the number. Product knows the roadmap. Finance knows the runway.

Your body — the one asset that has to outlast all of it — has no objective at all.

We don't guess. We decide what the quarter is for.

---

### 7. Direct question hook — closing register

When did you last review your training the way you review a P&L?

Look at what worked. Cut what didn't. Decide the next move.

I do that with clients on a schedule. Not when motivation dips. On a schedule.

That's the difference between a program and a hope.
