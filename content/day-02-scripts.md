# Day 2 Scripts — Systems

**Angle:** The 6am decision most operators get wrong: deciding what to do at the gym, instead of already knowing
**Platforms:** IG + TikTok
**Format:** Talking-head, direct to camera. Script 7 is built to pair with a text overlay of a real pre-built day.
**Hook types covered:** blunt statement, direct question, scenario, number-framed, contrarian claim, analogy

Read once, speak into the camera. No stage directions, no hashtags.

---

### 1. Blunt statement hook

You don't have a discipline problem. You have a decision problem.

You get to the gym at six. And that's when you start thinking about what to do.

Chest. Legs. Whatever you did last time.

By the time you decide, you've spent your sharpest twenty minutes deciding.

The plan should be finished before you walk in. We don't guess.

---

### 2. Direct question hook

What are you training tomorrow?

If you have to think about it, that's the problem.

Not the effort. Not the willpower. The fact that the decision is still open.

My clients know their next session before they go to bed. Exercise, order, weight, sets.

They don't decide. They execute.

---

### 3. Scenario hook

Six a.m. You get to the gym.

You stand in front of the rack and scroll your phone looking for something to do.

You've already lost.

Not the workout. The point of the workout.

The plan is supposed to be finished before you get there.

---

### 4. Number-framed hook — sharpest of the set, lead with this one

Three questions before you train. What am I doing. How heavy. In what order.

Most people answer all three standing in the gym.

I answer them before the week starts.

Same three questions. Different timing.

That's the whole difference.

---

### 5. Contrarian claim hook

Motivation isn't your bottleneck. Neither is time.

It's that you're doing the coach's job and the athlete's job at the same time.

Nobody performs well doing both.

Your job is to show up and execute. Deciding what is someone else's job.

---

### 6. Analogy hook

You don't walk into a board meeting and ask what the agenda is.

Someone built it. You run it.

Then you walk into the gym with no agenda at all and call it training.

Same person. Two standards.

---

### 7. Scenario hook — built for the text-overlay cut

This is Tuesday for one of my clients.

Six-fifteen. Incline dumbbell press. Four sets. Twenty, fifteen, twelve, ten.

Hammer curls between every set, while the press recovers.

He didn't pick any of that this morning. It was decided before the week started.

He showed up and did the work. Stay disciplined. Stay dominant.
