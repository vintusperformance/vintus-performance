# Vintus Performance — Launch Content Plan

Prepared by: Content Strategist
For: Anthony
Status: Ready to execute — no open questions, decisions made below

Context: pre-launch, zero audience, zero content history, solo founder, no ad
budget, no lead-gen tool. Organic content + Anthony's own outreach are the only
channels available. This plan is built for that reality, not an idealized one.

---

## 1. Content Pillars (launch-specific)

The five standing pillars, sharpened for a cold-start launch:

1. **Precision over guesswork** — the assessment/intake process, how a plan gets
   built, why "custom" means something specific here and not a marketing word.
2. **The high-performer problem** — training around travel, back-to-back
   calendars, and a job that doesn't stop. This is the pillar that makes the
   ICP feel *seen*, not sold to.
3. **Hybrid capability** — strength and endurance programmed together. Useful
   early because it's a real point of differentiation from both "bro" strength
   accounts and endurance-only coaching.
4. **Recovery as strategy** — readiness, load, sleep as inputs to the plan, not
   an afterthought. Signals sophistication without overclaiming the tech.
5. **Proof and process** — since there is no client history yet, this pillar is
   reframed for launch as *"what working with Vintus actually looks like"* —
   process transparency stands in for testimonials until real results exist.

No new pillars needed. The gap is capacity to produce, not a gap in the
framework.

## 2. Platform Priority

**Instagram leads at launch. LinkedIn is queued but on hold — Anthony's personal
profile needs polishing first, and Buffer isn't connected to LinkedIn yet (3-channel
plan limit already used by Instagram/TikTok/YouTube). Revisit LinkedIn once the
profile is ready; draft copy can be prepared in the meantime whenever there's
downtime. TikTok and YouTube stay held per the original reasoning below even though
both are already connected in Buffer.**

- **LinkedIn — primary.** This is the ICP's native habitat, and it's the one
  platform where a zero-follower founder account can still get distribution
  from day one — LinkedIn shows posts based on network and interest graph, not
  just existing following. Founder-voice, career/performance framing (per
  platform posture in CLAUDE.md) fits Anthony writing in his own voice with no
  production overhead. This is the highest-leverage channel for a cold start.
- **Instagram — secondary, launch immediately.** Needed as the visual storefront
  — it's where a prospect who found Anthony via LinkedIn, referral, or search
  goes to size him up before booking. Reels for discovery, grid/profile for
  credibility. Lower expectation of organic reach at zero followers, but it's
  infrastructure, not a growth bet, at this stage.
- **TikTok — hold.** The discovery algorithm over-indexes on general fitness
  audiences and hype-culture pacing, which cuts against the calm/precision
  brand voice and risks training the account toward the wrong audience early.
  Revisit once LinkedIn/IG cadence is proven sustainable and there's a backlog
  of clippable long-form content to draw from — not before.
- **YouTube — hold.** High trust yield but requires production overhead (scripting,
  filming, editing) disproportionate to what a solo founder can sustain while
  also running the business pre-launch. Revisit at month 2–3 once a short-form
  library exists to repurpose from, not as a from-scratch commitment now.

This is a deliberate two-platform start, not an oversight. Spreading across
four platforms at zero audience with one person producing everything is the
fastest way to produce nothing well.

## 3. Posting Cadence — First 4 Weeks

Realistic for a founder who is also closing the business's first clients.

| Week | LinkedIn | Instagram | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 posts | 2 Reels | Build the rhythm, don't overcommit before it's proven sustainable |
| 2 | 3 posts | 3 Reels | Step up once week 1 didn't break |
| 3 | 3 posts | 3 Reels | Hold steady |
| 4 | 3 posts | 3 Reels | Hold steady; review what resonated before planning month 2 |

Steady-state target from week 2 on: **3x/week per platform, 6 pieces of
content total per week.** Several ideas below are built to serve both
platforms in different formats (LinkedIn: text/carousel; IG: Reel/caption),
which keeps the real production load closer to 6–8 distinct ideas per week,
not 12.

Do not add TikTok, YouTube, or increase frequency until this cadence has run
clean for the full 4 weeks.

## 4. First-Batch Content Ideas (8–10, ready for script-writer)

Each includes pillar, platform, hook, offer tie-in, and a note on angle so
script-writer doesn't have to guess.

1. **"Not a diet. A precision system."**
   Pillar 1 · LinkedIn (text post) · Founder POV explaining what makes a
   Vintus plan different from a generic PDF program — the intake, the logic,
   the adjustment cadence. Ties to all training/nutrition plan tiers as the
   philosophy underneath them. Do not claim results — claim method.

2. **"The 3 questions I ask before I write anyone's plan"**
   Pillar 1 · Instagram Reel · Walks through the actual assessment inputs
   (goals, schedule constraints, training history) that shape a 30/60/90-day
   plan. Educational, not salesy — ends with a soft nod to the assessment
   flow on site.

3. **"You don't have a motivation problem. You have a scheduling problem."**
   Pillar 2 · LinkedIn (text post) · Thought-leadership framing for
   executives/founders — reframes "lack of discipline" as a design problem,
   not a willpower problem. Positions Private Coaching ($997/mo) as the
   answer to a structural problem, not a moral one.

4. **"Hybrid athlete doesn't mean doing two sports badly"**
   Pillar 3 · Instagram Reel, cross-post as LinkedIn carousel · Explains how
   strength and endurance are programmed to support each other rather than
   compete for recovery capacity. Ties to Private Coaching and longer plan
   tiers where periodization actually matters.

5. **"30, 60, or 90 days — how to know which one you actually need"**
   Pillar 1 · Instagram carousel · Direct offer-education piece. Maps plan
   length to real use cases (a reset, a full training block, a sustained
   overhaul) so the decision is legible instead of arbitrary. Clear CTA to
   the plan tiers on site.

6. **"Recovery isn't rest. It's data."**
   Pillar 4 · LinkedIn (text post) · Positions readiness and load management
   as inputs the plan actually uses, not wellness-brand language. Keep the
   tech reference (AI-assisted programming) understated — it should read as
   rigor, not gimmick.

7. **"Training around three weeks a month on the road"**
   Pillar 2 · Instagram Reel · Speaks directly to the travel-heavy exec/
   entrepreneur pain point — how a plan flexes for hotel gyms, time zones,
   and disrupted sleep without falling apart. Ties to Private Coaching and
   the nutrition plans (travel eating is the harder problem, not training).

8. **"How Vintus coaching is different from a generic online trainer"**
   Pillar 5 · LinkedIn (text post) · Direct positioning/differentiation piece.
   Explains what "private coaching" actually includes at $997/mo versus a
   templated plan — sets up the tier structure (one-time plans vs. ongoing
   coaching) without disparaging competitors by name.

9. **"Book 30 minutes with me before you commit to anything"**
   Pillar 5 · Instagram Reel + LinkedIn post (same idea, native to each) ·
   Direct, low-friction CTA to the $85 30-minute paid session. This is the
   most important post in the batch — with zero proof assets yet, a small
   paid conversation is the fastest way to convert cold interest into a real
   relationship. Frame as "come ask me anything, no pitch," consistent with
   "we don't guess."

10. **"What week one with Vintus actually looks like"**
    Pillar 5 · LinkedIn (text post) or Instagram carousel · Walks through the
    structure of a first week — assessment, plan delivery through the
    dashboard, first check-in — as a process explainer. This is the
    proof-and-process pillar doing its job without a single fabricated
    testimonial or claimed outcome. Explicitly frame as "here's what to
    expect," not "here's what happened."

## 5. Gaps — What We're Not Saying Yet (and should, later)

- **No pricing-transparency post.** This ICP responds to clarity, not mystery
  pricing. A clean "what this costs and why" post (tiers, what's included,
  no gimmicks) belongs in the queue by week 3–4, once the account has a
  little context built up — going pricing-first at post #1 would read as a
  sales page before there's any trust.
- **No explanation of the AI-assisted coaching mechanism.** "AI-assisted"
  is a real differentiator but also a real risk if introduced clumsily — it
  can read as less personal to a premium audience if led with. Hold this for
  a dedicated, carefully-framed piece once the account has an audience that
  already trusts Anthony's judgment — not in the first batch.
- **No objection-handling content.** "Isn't this just ChatGPT with a workout
  plan," "why not just hire a normal trainer" — real objections this audience
  will have. Deliberately not in batch one; addressing objections before
  anyone's asked them reads defensive. Queue for month 2 once DMs/comments
  surface the actual objections in Anthony's own words.
- **No asset library exists.** There's no client footage, no b-roll, no
  photo library — everything in this batch has to be Anthony on camera or
  text-first. That's a real production constraint, not a strategy gap, but
  it caps format variety until `creative-director` and Anthony build a basic
  shot list. Flagging it now so it doesn't surprise anyone in week 2.

## 6. What Success Looks Like in 30 Days

Realistic, not hype — and deliberately not revenue-attributed, since organic
content from zero doesn't convert to $997/mo signups in one month.

- **Consistency, not virality.** All 22 scheduled posts (10 LinkedIn + 10 IG
  across weeks 2–4, plus 4 in week 1) actually shipped on schedule. The
  system holding up under a busy founder's calendar is itself the win.
- **Early distribution signals, not followers.** Impressions, saves, and
  profile visits trending up post-over-post — not a follower-count target.
  At zero audience, follower count this early is noise.
- **First inbound signal.** At least a handful of DMs, comments, or direct
  questions that indicate the message is landing with the right kind of
  person — not the volume, the *fit*.
- **The 30-minute paid session as the real conversion metric.** 1–3 booked
  $85 sessions attributable to organic content is a legitimate, honest win
  for month one. That's the correct-sized ask for a cold audience — not a
  $997/mo signup.
- **A clear read on what resonated.** Enough signal by day 30 to know which
  pillar and format (e.g., travel-training vs. hybrid-capability, Reel vs.
  text post) earned the most engagement, so month 2 doubles down instead of
  guessing again.

If day 30 shows steady posting, rising impressions, and a couple of booked
paid sessions — that's the plan working. A Private Coaching signup in month
one would be a bonus, not the bar.
