# Instagram Carousel — "30, 60, or 90 days"

**Idea #5** · Pillar 1 (Precision over guesswork) · Static graphic carousel, no filming
**Format:** 6 slides + caption
**CTA destination:** Training plan tiers on vintusperformance.org (via the assessment)

---

## Slide 1 — Hook

**Visual:** Bold headline on brand background. No body copy beyond this line.

> Most people pick 30, 60, or 90 days by guessing.
> Here's how to actually know.

---

## Slide 2 — The 30-Day Plan

**Visual:** "30 DAYS" as the slide's numeral/anchor, minimal supporting text.

> **30 days is a reset.**
> You know what to do. You stopped doing it.
> This is a short, structured block to get back under the bar and rebuild the habit — not a transformation window.

---

## Slide 3 — The 60-Day Plan

**Visual:** "60 DAYS" as the anchor.

> **60 days is a training block.**
> Long enough to actually progress a lift, a pace, or a body composition goal — not just feel busy.
> This is the tier for someone training toward something specific with a deadline in mind.

---

## Slide 4 — The 90-Day Plan

**Visual:** "90 DAYS" as the anchor.

> **90 days is a sustained overhaul.**
> Enough time to periodize — build a base, push a peak, and back off before it breaks you.
> This is for someone rebuilding capacity from the ground up, not patching one weak spot.

---

## Slide 5 — The Decision Framework

**Visual:** Simple 3-line recap, could be a clean table or stacked text.

> **Reset → 30 days**
> **Progress a goal → 60 days**
> **Rebuild capacity → 90 days**
>
> Match the length to the problem. Not the other way around.

---

## Slide 6 — Close / CTA

**Visual:** Clean brand close, logo mark or wordmark, single CTA line.

> We don't guess — and you shouldn't either.
> Pick your length. The assessment at vintusperformance.org is what your program gets built from.

## Caption

Picking a plan length by feel is how people quit week two of a 90-day block they never needed.

Here's the actual logic:

→ Need to get back under the bar? **30 days.**
→ Chasing one specific goal with a real deadline? **60 days.**
→ Rebuilding capacity from the ground up? **90 days.**

Each one is built for a different problem — not a different level of commitment. Once you know which problem is yours, the assessment at vintusperformance.org is where your program gets built.

Stay disciplined. Stay dominant.
