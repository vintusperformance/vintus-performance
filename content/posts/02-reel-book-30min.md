## Full Script

**[ON CAMERA — cold open, no intro, straight into the hook]**

**HOOK (use variant A):**
"Don't sign up for anything with me yet. Book 30 minutes first."

**[BEAT — problem, stated precisely]**
"Right now, everything you know about Vintus is what's on a website. You don't
know how I actually think about training. You don't know if I'll tell you the
truth when the truth isn't flattering. And you shouldn't hand over $997 a month
to find out."

**[BEAT — the mechanism / why this exists]**
"So instead of asking you to trust a page, I built a smaller door in. Thirty
minutes, on a call, with me. Not a sales rep. Me."

**[BEAT — the correction / what it actually is]**
"Bring your actual situation — your schedule, your training history, whatever's
not working right now. Ask me anything. How I'd program around your travel.
Whether you need a plan or ongoing coaching. Whether this is even the right fit
at all. I'll tell you straight, including if the answer is no."

**[BEAT — reframe the ask honestly]**
"This isn't a pitch call. There's no slide deck. If it's not a fit, you'll know
in thirty minutes, and you'll have paid $85 to know it instead of guessing."

**[CTA — single, specific, on screen and spoken]**
"Link's in the bio. Book the 30-minute session at vintusperformance.org/paid-session.
Come with your real questions."

## Shot / Framing Notes (solo filming, no crew)

- **Setup:** Phone on tripod, eye level with camera — not shooting up or down.
  Frame chest-up, some headroom, centered or slight rule-of-thirds offset.
- **Location:** Wherever reads as real working environment — home gym, office,
  or training floor. Avoid a backdrop that looks staged or salesy. Natural light
  from a window in front of or beside Anthony, not behind (avoid silhouette).
- **Audio:** Use a clip-on/lav mic if available. Room tone should be quiet —
  this script relies on the words landing, not on music carrying it.
- **Pacing:** One continuous take is fine and preferred — this reads more
  credible unscripted than over-cut. If cutting for pacing, cut on hard stops
  between beats (after "Me." / after "no." / before the CTA), not mid-sentence.
- **On-screen text:** Overlay the hook line as text for the first 1–2 seconds
  (sound-off viewers). Overlay "$85 · 30 minutes · vintusperformance.org/paid-session"
  during the CTA beat.
- **Tone on camera:** Calm, direct eye contact with the lens, no forced energy.
  This should feel like a private conversation, not a pitch — matching the
  script's own framing.
- **Length target:** 30–40 seconds. Cut anything that doesn't move hook →
  problem → mechanism → correction → CTA.
