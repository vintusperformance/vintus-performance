# Day 3 Scripts — Hybrid Performance

**Angle:** Why I stopped optimizing for one number and built general capacity instead — the same mistake I see in operators who only optimize revenue
**Platforms:** IG + TikTok
**Format:** Talking-head + training b-roll
**Repurpose source:** the "gym = incomplete" post (07/25), rewritten with an explicit business-operator analogy instead of pure fitness-identity framing
**Hook types covered:** blunt statement, direct question, scenario, contrarian claim, analogy, number-framed

Read once, speak into the camera. No stage directions, no hashtags.

---

### 1. Blunt statement hook

I used to train for one number. My bench.

It went up. My conditioning fell apart.

I optimized one metric and let the system rot around it.

You've watched a company do the same thing with revenue.

---

### 2. Direct question hook

What's your one number?

Revenue. Headcount. Whatever you check first every morning.

Now — what got worse while you were making it go up?

I did this with a barbell for two years. Same mistake. Smaller stakes.

---

### 3. Scenario hook

A company doubles revenue. Margin collapses. Support drowns. Two senior people quit.

The number went up. The business got worse.

That is what training for one lift looks like inside a body.

---

### 4. Contrarian claim hook

Being strong isn't the goal. Being hard to break is.

The strongest person in the gym is often the least capable one outside it.

General capacity beats a single number.

In a body and in a business.

---

### 5. Number-framed hook — sharpest of the set, lead with this one

I check four things before I write anyone's week. Sleep. Stress. Training load. Schedule.

Not one of them is a lift number.

Because the lift is an output. Those four are the inputs.

Fix the inputs. The output follows.

---

### 6. Analogy hook

A business with one revenue line isn't strong. It's exposed.

A body with one trained quality is the same.

Strength. Conditioning. Mobility. Recovery.

Four lines instead of one. That isn't doing more. That is refusing to be fragile.

---

### 7. Scenario hook — pair with race or training b-roll

Ironman taught me something I didn't expect.

The race isn't won by the best swimmer, or the best cyclist, or the best runner.

It's won by the person with no weak leg.

Your week works the same way.

You don't need a standout quality. You need no collapsing one.
