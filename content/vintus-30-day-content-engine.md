## Part 1 — Target Audience

Not "busy professionals." The person Anthony is talking to on camera:

**Who, specifically:** 35-50 years old. Founder, partner, VP, or senior operator —
runs a company, a P&L, or a team where their output is directly visible and
directly compensated. Revenue responsibility or headcount responsibility, usually
both. Travels for work at least monthly. Income supports $997/mo without a
second thought if the value is obviously real — this is not a stretch purchase,
it's a rounding error against what a bad week at work costs them.

**Their relationship to fitness today:** Not a beginner. They already train —
4-5x/week, often with real athletic history (college sport, a marathon, a triathlon
attempt, a home gym). The problem isn't inactivity, it's that training is the one
part of their life still run on improvisation. Every other domain — calendar,
finances, hiring, travel — is systematized, delegated, or outsourced. Training is
the exception: they wake up and decide what to do that day, the same way they
haven't operated any other part of their life since their twenties.

**What they're actually struggling with:**
- Decision fatigue by the time they get to their own health — they've spent all
  day making calls for other people and have nothing left to plan their own workout.
- Inconsistency driven by travel and schedule volatility, not lack of will.
- A nagging sense that they're "good, not optimized" — they look fine, but they
  suspect their energy, recovery, and focus are quietly capped by an unmanaged
  training and sleep load.
- Low tolerance for being talked down to. They've been sold to by trainers who
  either treat them like a client with unlimited time, or hype them up like a
  new gym member. Neither lands.

**What they actually want:** Not abs. Energy that holds through a 12-hour day.
Focus that doesn't crater by 3pm. Confidence that their body will hold up as
long as their career demands. The ability to walk into a boardroom and a
starting line with the same standard. They want someone to own the *thinking*
so they only have to own the *doing* — this is the exact "Anthony owns the
strategy, the client owns the execution" trade CLAUDE.md defines the $997/mo
offer around.

**Where their head is at when they'd actually buy:** They're not looking for
motivation — they have plenty. They're looking for evidence someone understands
how their week actually works (travel, meetings, energy crashes, inconsistent
sleep) and will build around it instead of handing them a generic plan. They
decide fast once they trust the operator. They are turned off by hype, by
"look at my six pack," and by anything that reads as a 24-year-old personal
trainer's Instagram. They are reassured by precision, specificity, and restraint.

**Test for every piece of content in this calendar:** would this specific person —
mid-40s founder, mid-flight to a client meeting, phone in hand for 40 seconds —
stop scrolling and think "this guy gets my actual problem"? If the honest answer
is "no, but a 22-year-old gym-goer would love this," it doesn't belong here,
regardless of how well a similar post performed in the raw metrics below.

## Part 3 — The Daily "Content" Protocol

When Anthony types `content` (or similar shorthand — "today's content," "next
day," etc.), the responding session should:

1. **Locate progress.** Check this file plus whatever posting log/Buffer state
   is available to determine the last completed day of the 30. If none posted
   yet, start at Day 1.
2. **Pull the next slot(s).** Default to producing scripts for exactly the next
   unused day. If Anthony is visibly behind schedule (multiple days elapsed
   with no post), pull the next 2 days, not more — do not try to "catch up" in
   one dump that's unusable in a single sitting.
3. **Hand off to `script-writer`**, not write the scripts inline. Pass it:
   the day's pillar, angle, platform(s), format, and — for repurpose days —
   the named source post and the specific new lens (Q4 strategy, recovery/load
   management, etc.) so it isn't guessing intent. `script-writer` produces
   5-10 ready-to-speak scripts for that day's angle, each under ~60 seconds
   spoken, in Vintus brand voice: short declarative sentences, calm/disciplined/
   confident, no hype language, no emojis, established lines
   ("Stay disciplined. Stay dominant." / "Your only competition is yesterday."
   / "Not a diet. A precision system." / "We don't guess.") used only where
   they land naturally, not forced into every script.
4. **Hand off to `short-form-optimizer`** after script-writer drafts, to
   sharpen hooks and pacing for IG/TikTok retention. It sharpens, it does not
   write from scratch.
5. **Mark the day used** by updating this file's calendar (append a ✅ or a
   short note next to the day row) so the next `content` session knows where
   to resume. This file is the persistent memory for the 30-day push — future
   sessions read it instead of re-deriving strategy, pillar mix, or the
   repurposing plan from scratch.
6. **Flag drift, don't silently fix it.** If a produced script reads as pure
   hybrid-athlete content with no career/executive framing, or leans on hype
   language, send it back rather than shipping it — this is the exact failure
   mode CLAUDE.md names as the thing content has been getting wrong.

## Gaps — What This Plan Doesn't Cover

- **No direct-offer or objection-handling content.** Nothing here explicitly
  addresses "why is this different from a $150/mo trainer app" or names the
  $997/mo price. That's intentional for a pure-authority month, but it means
  this 30 days builds trust without building a conversion path — `sales-agent`
  and `funnel-optimization` should own whether a light CTA belongs on Days
  28-30 once Anthony sees how the month performs.
- **No wearable/schedule-aware proof.** CLAUDE.md is explicit that
  device integrations are stubbed, not live. Content should keep claiming the
  *philosophy* ("built around your actual calendar") without implying the
  product currently ingests live sleep/travel data — don't let a script
  overclaim what device.service.ts can't yet do.
- **LinkedIn is flagged, not fully planned.** The LI tags above identify good
  candidates for a text-first LinkedIn repost, but this calendar doesn't
  write native LinkedIn copy — that's a `script-writer` pass once Anthony
  confirms he wants LinkedIn running in parallel to the daily IG/TikTok push,
  not folded into the same 30-day cadence.
- **No YouTube.** All 30 days are short-form. If long-form authority content
  (the platform CLAUDE.md flags as "lower volume, higher trust yield") is
  wanted this month, it needs a separate, smaller-cadence plan — bolting it
  onto a daily short-form push is not realistic for a founder-operated shoot
  schedule.
