## Part 1 — Target Audience

Not "busy professionals." The person Anthony is talking to on camera:

**Who, specifically:** 35-50 years old. Founder, partner, VP, or senior operator —
runs a company, a P&L, or a team where their output is directly visible and
directly compensated. Revenue responsibility or headcount responsibility, usually
both. Travels for work at least monthly. Income supports $997/mo without a
second thought if the value is obviously real — this is not a stretch purchase,
it's a rounding error against what a bad week at work costs them.

**Their relationship to fitness today:** Not a beginner. They already train —
4-5x/week, often with real athletic history (college sport, a marathon, a triathlon
attempt, a home gym). The problem isn't inactivity, it's that training is the one
part of their life still run on improvisation. Every other domain — calendar,
finances, hiring, travel — is systematized, delegated, or outsourced. Training is
the exception: they wake up and decide what to do that day, the same way they
haven't operated any other part of their life since their twenties.

**What they're actually struggling with:**
- Decision fatigue by the time they get to their own health — they've spent all
  day making calls for other people and have nothing left to plan their own workout.
- Inconsistency driven by travel and schedule volatility, not lack of will.
- A nagging sense that they're "good, not optimized" — they look fine, but they
  suspect their energy, recovery, and focus are quietly capped by an unmanaged
  training and sleep load.
- Low tolerance for being talked down to. They've been sold to by trainers who
  either treat them like a client with unlimited time, or hype them up like a
  new gym member. Neither lands.

**What they actually want:** Not abs. Energy that holds through a 12-hour day.
Focus that doesn't crater by 3pm. Confidence that their body will hold up as
long as their career demands. The ability to walk into a boardroom and a
starting line with the same standard. They want someone to own the *thinking*
so they only have to own the *doing* — this is the exact "Anthony owns the
strategy, the client owns the execution" trade CLAUDE.md defines the $997/mo
offer around.

**Where their head is at when they'd actually buy:** They're not looking for
motivation — they have plenty. They're looking for evidence someone understands
how their week actually works (travel, meetings, energy crashes, inconsistent
sleep) and will build around it instead of handing them a generic plan. They
decide fast once they trust the operator. They are turned off by hype, by
"look at my six pack," and by anything that reads as a 24-year-old personal
trainer's Instagram. They are reassured by precision, specificity, and restraint.

**Test for every piece of content in this calendar:** would this specific person —
mid-40s founder, mid-flight to a client meeting, phone in hand for 40 seconds —
stop scrolling and think "this guy gets my actual problem"? If the honest answer
is "no, but a 22-year-old gym-goer would love this," it doesn't belong here,
regardless of how well a similar post performed in the raw metrics below.

## Part 2 — 30-Day Calendar

Pillar mix (target, holds within +/-1 across 30 days): Executive Performance 12
(40%) · Systems 8 (25%→27%) · Hybrid Performance 6 (20%) · Leadership Through
Health 4 (13%→15%).

**Platform default:** every day posts to IG + TikTok (same cut, native caption
per platform — short punchy captions outperformed long ones in the data, keep
IG captions under 2 lines). Days flagged **LI** are also strong candidates for a
rewritten, text-first LinkedIn post (no video re-edit needed) per CLAUDE.md's
platform posture that LinkedIn is the ICP's native habitat — career framing over
gym framing translates directly to a LinkedIn caption with minimal extra work.

**Repurpose engine (Anthony's explicit ask):** four real top-performers get
re-cut into new angles across the month, each time reframed through Executive
Performance / Systems / Leadership — never re-posted as-is, and never left as
pure hybrid-athlete content with zero career framing. Repurpose days are marked.

**Soft CTA policy — Days 25-30 (approved by Anthony, Aug 11, per
`creator-analysis.md`'s finding that no studied creator sustains zero CTA for 30
days):** these six closing days each carry a low-friction, non-pressuring next
step — a follow prompt or a bio-link mention. This is **not** a hard pitch and
**never** names the offer or the price — it exists only to remove the total
absence of a next step across the month, not to convert. Days 1-24 stay exactly
as designed: pure authority, no CTA. The specific CTA line for each of the six
days is in that row's Notes column below; `script-writer` should treat it as
direction, not a rigid script, but keep the register identical (no urgency, no
"link in bio NOW," no offer language).

**Branded-phrase policy (approved by Anthony, Aug 11, per `creator-analysis.md`'s
finding that identity/in-group phrases work through repetition, not a single
use):** CLAUDE.md's positioning sentence — "We build performance systems for
people whose careers don't allow average health." — now recurs **4 times** across
the month instead of only once at the Day 30 capstone: **Days 5, 15, 22, and 30**,
spread across the month by design (not clustered) so it reads as a recurring
brand line the audience starts to recognize, not a slogan dropped once at the
end. Each occurrence is marked in that row's Notes column and is written to land
as the natural closing line for that day's specific angle — `script-writer`
should not force it in if a given script's draft doesn't have a clean place for
it; flag back rather than wedging it in awkwardly.

## Part 3 — The Daily "Content" Protocol

When Anthony types `content` (or similar shorthand — "today's content," "next
day," etc.), the responding session should:

1. **Locate progress.** Check this file plus whatever posting log/Buffer state
   is available to determine the last completed day of the 30. If none posted
   yet, start at Day 1.
2. **Pull the next slot(s).** Default to producing scripts for exactly the next
   unused day. If Anthony is visibly behind schedule (multiple days elapsed
   with no post), pull the next 2 days, not more — do not try to "catch up" in
   one dump that's unusable in a single sitting.
3. **Hand off to `script-writer`**, not write the scripts inline. Pass it:
   the day's pillar, angle, platform(s), format, and — for repurpose days —
   the named source post and the specific new lens (Q4 strategy, recovery/load
   management, etc.) so it isn't guessing intent. For Days 25-30 also pass the
   day's specific soft-CTA line, and for Days 5/15/22/30 pass the branded-phrase
   placement (see Part 2's policies and the calendar's Notes column). `script-writer`
   produces 5-10 ready-to-speak scripts for that day's angle, each under ~60
   seconds spoken, in Vintus brand voice: short declarative sentences, calm/
   disciplined/confident, no hype language, no emojis, established lines
   ("Stay disciplined. Stay dominant." / "Your only competition is yesterday."
   / "Not a diet. A precision system." / "We don't guess.") used only where
   they land naturally, not forced into every script.
   - **Hook-type checklist (mandatory every day, not just Day 1).** Tag each
     script variant with one of the six hook types `day-01-scripts.md`
     established — **blunt statement, direct question, scenario, number-framed,
     contrarian claim, analogy** — and treat covering multiple types across the
     day's 5-10 scripts as a deliberate production requirement. Source:
     `creator-analysis.md`'s cross-cutting finding that every well-documented
     creator studied treats the hook as an engineered production step, not an
     accident — this formalizes that as a standing checklist item rather than
     something that happened once, incidentally, on Day 1.
   - **Process-specificity (mandatory).** Every script should lead with, or
     clearly contain, a specific, checkable process claim — what Anthony
     actually asks, checks, or does, in order (e.g. "I ask three questions
     before I program week one," "sleep, then stress, then load, in that
     order" — already the shape of Days 10/21/28). This is Vintus's brand-safe
     substitute for the proof/stats/testimonial lever other creators lean on:
     Vintus has no verified client results to cite (CLAUDE.md Standing Rule 2),
     so specificity of *process* is the trust lever instead of specificity of
     *outcome*. A script with a sharp hook but only vague inspiration behind it
     isn't finished — send it back for a concrete process claim.
4. **Hand off to `short-form-optimizer`** after script-writer drafts, to
   sharpen hooks and pacing for IG/TikTok retention. It sharpens, it does not
   write from scratch.
5. **Hand off to `brand-director`** for the pass CLAUDE.md's orchestration
   rules require before anything client-facing ships. Run the **"no flex"
   check** specifically: does the script sell the *outcome* (energy, focus, a
   standard held) or the *appearance of success* (lifestyle signaling,
   physique, income/status flex)? Only the former belongs in Vintus content.
   This check exists directly because of `creator-analysis.md`'s finding that
   lifestyle-flex and physique-as-product are structurally common tactics in
   the broader creator-economy playbook but are named there as flatly
   incompatible with Vintus brand voice — CLAUDE.md states outright that
   "executives don't want abs." Reject and send back rather than quietly
   editing around it.
6. **Mark the day used** by updating this file's calendar (append a ✅ or a
   short note next to the day row) so the next `content` session knows where
   to resume. This file is the persistent memory for the 30-day push — future
   sessions read it instead of re-deriving strategy, pillar mix, or the
   repurposing plan from scratch.
7. **Flag drift, don't silently fix it.** If a produced script reads as pure
   hybrid-athlete content with no career/executive framing, leans on hype
   language, or fails the no-flex check in step 5, send it back rather than
   shipping it — this is the exact failure mode CLAUDE.md names as the thing
   content has been getting wrong.

## Gaps — What This Plan Doesn't Cover

- **No direct-offer or objection-handling content.** Nothing here explicitly
  addresses "why is this different from a $150/mo trainer app" or names the
  $997/mo price. That's intentional for a pure-authority month. **Update
  (Aug 11):** Anthony reviewed `creator-analysis.md`'s recommendations and
  approved a soft, low-friction CTA (follow prompt or bio-link mention only —
  never an offer or price) for Days 25-30 specifically, to remove the total
  absence of a next step across the month per that file's finding that no
  studied creator sustains zero CTA for 30 days straight. See the Part 2
  calendar rows for the specific line per day. This still isn't a conversion
  path — `sales-agent` and `funnel-optimization` still own whether an actual
  offer or objection-handling layer belongs in a future month's plan.
- **No wearable/schedule-aware proof.** CLAUDE.md is explicit that
  device integrations are stubbed, not live. Content should keep claiming the
  *philosophy* ("built around your actual calendar") without implying the
  product currently ingests live sleep/travel data — don't let a script
  overclaim what device.service.ts can't yet do.
- **LinkedIn is flagged, not fully planned.** The LI tags above identify good
  candidates for a text-first LinkedIn repost, but this calendar doesn't
  write native LinkedIn copy — that's a `script-writer` pass once Anthony
  confirms he wants LinkedIn running in parallel to the daily IG/TikTok push,
  not folded into the same 30-day cadence.
- **No YouTube.** All 30 days are short-form. If long-form authority content
  (the platform CLAUDE.md flags as "lower volume, higher trust yield") is
  wanted this month, it needs a separate, smaller-cadence plan — bolting it
  onto a daily short-form push is not realistic for a founder-operated shoot
  schedule.
- **Andre Contreras and "It Is Joao" research is closed, not paused.**
  `creator-analysis.md` left both creators' content specifics unresolved:
  Contreras was dropped by Anthony's explicit choice ("that's all that
  matters, let's move forward," Aug 11), and itsjoao (@it.is.joao) stayed
  blocked by Instagram's access limits — no authenticated fetch is possible
  in this environment, and public search couldn't independently surface a
  reliable breakdown of that specific account's content niche or hook style.
  Future sessions should **not** re-attempt this research unprompted. If
  either becomes relevant again, it needs a fresh, explicit ask from Anthony —
  an exact handle for Contreras, or pasted reel transcripts/screenshots for
  itsjoao — not another round of speculative search.
