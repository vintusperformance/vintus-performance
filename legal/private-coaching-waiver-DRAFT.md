>
> This version is grounded in actual New Jersey law rather than generic boilerplate,
> since Vintus Performance is based in Morristown, NJ:
>
> - **Pre-injury liability waivers for fitness/exercise negligence are enforceable in
>   New Jersey**, per the NJ Supreme Court's *Stelluti v. Casapenn Enterprises, LLC*
>   (2010) — provided the release is clear, unambiguous, not buried in fine print, and
>   reflects a voluntary, informed decision. Section 6 below is written to match that
>   standard.
> - **New Jersey will not enforce a waiver of gross negligence, recklessness, or
>   intentional misconduct**, regardless of wording. Section 6's carve-out for the
>   Released Parties' own gross negligence/willful misconduct isn't a hedge — it's
>   required for the rest of the release to hold up at all.
> - **New Jersey's Health Club Services Act (N.J.S.A. 56:8-39 et seq.) voids liability
>   waivers outright for any business the statute defines as a "health club."** That
>   definition turns on operating a physical facility ("devotes at least 40% of its
>   facility to..."). Vintus has no physical facility clients train at — it's
>   remote/virtual coaching delivered through a dashboard — which is a real, reasoned
>   basis that the Act doesn't apply here. This is the one inference in this document
>   that isn't a settled legal fact; if Vintus ever adds a physical location or
>   in-person group sessions, revisit this.

## 2. Assumption of Risk

You understand that participation in physical exercise of any kind carries inherent
risks, including but not limited to: musculoskeletal injury (sprains, strains, tears,
fractures); cardiovascular events, including in rare cases heart attack or sudden
cardiac events, particularly in individuals with undiagnosed or undisclosed heart
conditions; aggravation of pre-existing injuries or conditions; and, in rare cases,
permanent disability or death. These risks exist even when a program is properly
designed and properly followed, and may be heightened by factors you have not
disclosed to Vintus.

You voluntarily and knowingly assume all such risks, known and unknown, arising from
your participation in the Program, including risks arising from the ordinary
negligence of Vintus, its owners, employees, contractors, and coaches, to the fullest
extent permitted under New Jersey law. This assumption of risk does not extend to
gross negligence, recklessness, or intentional misconduct, which New Jersey law does
not permit to be waived.

## 3. Medical Clearance and Health Representations

You represent and warrant that:

a. You have no medical condition, injury, or physical limitation that would make
   participation in a strength and conditioning program unsafe, **except as you have
   specifically disclosed to Vintus in your intake assessment**;
b. You are voluntarily participating and have either consulted a physician about
   beginning an exercise and/or nutrition program, or you are knowingly declining to
   do so at your own risk;
c. You will immediately stop any exercise and seek appropriate medical attention if you
   experience chest pain, dizziness, shortness of breath disproportionate to effort,
   or any other warning sign, rather than continuing or asking Jerry to advise you
   through it; and
d. All information you provide to Vintus — injury history, medical conditions,
   medications, and physical capabilities — is accurate and complete, and you
   understand that the program built for you relies on that information being true.

**Vintus is entitled to rely on the accuracy of what you disclose.** Programming is
built around your reported history; it cannot account for a condition you did not
report.

## 4. AI-Assisted Coaching Disclosure

Some or all of your training program, nutrition guidance, and day-to-day coaching
messages are generated or drafted with the assistance of an artificial intelligence
system, which may be reviewed by a human coach before or after delivery depending on
your service tier. You understand that:

a. AI-generated guidance, including messages from "Jerry," is not a substitute for
   professional medical, psychological, or dietetic advice;
b. Jerry is instructed not to diagnose conditions or give specific medical advice, and

   actually follow that direction rather than treating Jerry's general guidance as a
   medical opinion; and
c. You may request adjustments to your program at any time, but any change you request
   is implemented based on the information you provide at that time.

## 5. No Guarantee of Results

Vintus does not guarantee any specific result, including weight loss, muscle gain,
strength gain, performance improvement, or any aesthetic or health outcome. Results
depend on factors outside Vintus's control, including your adherence, genetics,
sleep, stress, nutrition compliance outside prescribed guidance, and disclosed and
undisclosed health conditions.

## 6. Release and Waiver of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted under New Jersey law, you release, waive, discharge,
and covenant not to sue Vintus Performance LLC, its owners, officers, employees,
contractors, and coaching staff (collectively, the "Released Parties") from any and
all liability, claims, demands, actions, or causes of action whatsoever arising out of
or related to any loss, damage, or injury, including death, that may be sustained by
you, or to any property belonging to you, while participating in the Program,
**whether arising from the ordinary negligence of the Released Parties or otherwise**,
except to the extent caused by the Released Parties' gross negligence, recklessness,
or willful misconduct. This carve-out is required for this release to be enforceable
under New Jersey law (*Stelluti v. Casapenn Enters., LLC*, 203 N.J. 286 (2010)) — it
is not optional and should not be narrowed.

damage, or cost (including reasonable attorneys' fees) they may incur due to your

Your participation in the Program, and in any specific workout or nutrition
recommendation within it, is voluntary. You may decline, modify, or stop any exercise
or plan at any time. Nothing in this Agreement obligates you to perform an exercise
